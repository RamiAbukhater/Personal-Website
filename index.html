Current Projects & Roles

What I am building right now, across industry, student teams, and my own research.

Selene Nanorobotics, Sensing & Navigation for Root-Canal Microrobots

Selene is building magnetically steered microrobots that clear bacterial biofilm from inside root canals, places conventional treatment struggles to reach. I lead sensor simulation and navigation, building the simulator that works out how to tell where a robot is inside the canal, and turning it into a tool that searches for better robot designs and sensor layouts. Specifics are under NDA.

Triton AI, Perception for an Autonomous Vehicle

On UC San Diego's student autonomy team, I am building the perception stack that lets our vehicle see. I brought up its first full sensing suite, front and rear cameras plus a 360° LiDAR calibrated into one synchronized view of the world, the foundation the rest of the autonomy stack builds on.

DataWorks, Audio-Perception Lab

I lead an audio-perception lab inside UC San Diego's student data science organization. We are building a shared pipeline that turns raw sound into tokens a multimodal transformer can read, then testing whether one general model can match classifiers built for a single task.

See my role →

Why We Procrastinate, Reinforcement Learning Research

In Prof. Lucy Lai's group in UC San Diego Cognitive Science, which studies how people learn and decide with limited cognitive resources, I am asking why people put things off. I am rewriting the Bellman equation at the heart of reinforcement learning so agents procrastinate the way people do, then fitting them to human data to test whether discounting the future is enough to explain it.

VIA, Cognitively-Grounded Vision-Language-Action Model

A robot-learning model that reasons instead of reacting. Rather than mapping camera frames straight to actions, VIA keeps a probabilistic belief about the world, infers the goal behind a language instruction, and mentally simulates options before it acts.
